Site Help — Updating PPOTY by Email

Everything on the dashboard (results, schedule, photos, the scrolling message) is updated by sending an email to the intake address. This page is the full cheat sheet: every keyword, what it does, and how the confirmation step works.

How it works, in short

  1. Send an email to the intake address (from an approved sender — see below).
  2. Put a keyword as the first line of either the subject or the body — see the keyword list below.
  3. The system checks the inbox every few minutes. If it understood you, it replies with a summary of what it's about to do and asks for confirmation.
  4. Reply YES to publish it, or NO to discard it. Nothing goes live until you reply YES.
  5. Once confirmed, most updates appear on the live site within a couple of minutes.
Only email addresses on the approved-sender list can trigger updates. Anyone else emailing the intake address is silently ignored (no reply, no error) — this is intentional so it can't be abused. Ask whoever runs the automation to add an address if it needs to be able to make updates.

Keywords

Photo by itself — add to the gallery

Attach a photo with no special keyword in the subject or body. It's added straight to the site's photo gallery — no YES/NO confirmation needed, it publishes immediately.

RESULTS — publish a game's results

Put RESULTS as the first line of the subject or body, and attach a photo of the payouts (a Tournament Director screen, a printed sheet, or handwritten notes all work). The system reads the photo, matches player names to the roster, and drafts a game entry.

It replies with a summary (date, pot, places, payouts) — check it over, then reply YES to publish or NO to discard. If the photo can't be read clearly, it'll ask for a clearer one instead of guessing.

Subject: RESULTS (attach photo of the payout sheet)

SCHEDULE: — add a new game to the schedule

Put SCHEDULE: followed by the details (name, date, time, place — plain English is fine) as the first line of the subject or body.

SCHEDULE: PPOTN Night, August 8 2026, 7pm, Stuart's House

It replies with the parsed event — reply YES to add it to the schedule sidebar on the site, or NO to discard.

SCHEDULE CHANGE: — edit or cancel an existing game

Put SCHEDULE CHANGE: followed by the date of the game you're changing (as it appears on the site) and either what's changing (new time, new place, new date) or the word cancel to remove it entirely.

SCHEDULE CHANGE: August 8 2026, moved to 8pm SCHEDULE CHANGE: August 8 2026, cancel

It matches your message to the existing entry by date. If it can't find a match, it replies with the current schedule so you can try again with the exact date. Reply YES to confirm the edit or cancellation, NO to leave the schedule as-is.

MESSAGE: — set the scrolling banner on the site

Put MESSAGE: followed by whatever you want the scrolling banner to say, as the first line of the subject or body.

MESSAGE: Reminder — bring cash for next week's buy-in!

To remove the current banner, send MESSAGE: clear (or none / remove). Reply YES to confirm either way.

Replying YES or NO

Whatever you're confirming, reply to that same email thread with YES or NO as the very first line of your reply (before any quoted text below it).

You reply withWhat happens
YES or YPublishes the change to the live site.
NO or NDiscards it — nothing changes.

Gmail's "Smart Reply" suggestion buttons (e.g. "Yes!", "No for me.", "No, sorry.") are also recognized — it only looks at the first word of your reply. If you're ever unsure, just type a plain YES or NO yourself.

If you don't reply YES or NO, the request just sits there — it'll gently remind you once, but it won't nag repeatedly, and it never publishes anything on its own.

Notifications

When results are published or the schedule is added to / changed / cancelled, everyone on the notification list gets a short email with a link to the site. Photos and the scrolling message don't trigger a notification.

Multiple people making updates

More than one email address can be approved to send updates — it's a simple list, so an admin or a second trusted person can be added the same way as any other approved sender. Each person's replies (YES/NO) only affect the thread they're replying to, so there's no conflict between people working on different updates at the same time.
