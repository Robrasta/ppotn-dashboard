Scenario Modeler

What-if finishes · all 12 players

Model one hypothetical game with every player at once: pick a finish for as many of the 12 members as you like — say Rob 1st and James 5th — and the standings below recalculate for everyone. The pot is built from a full game: every member counts toward the pot unless you mark them “Not in game”, plus any guests — so a 1st-place finish pays like a real PPOTN night, not like a two-player game. Change any finish and run it again as many times as you want; nothing here is saved or affects the real standings.
